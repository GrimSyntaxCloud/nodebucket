THE ORIGINAL STORY

It started with
an overflowing list.

In the fictional Nodebucket story, Thor “that’s his name” Odinson and Tom “normal guy” Smith headed to the mountains in 2007 to make sense of the endless tasks of everyday life. Sam “the tech guy” Fox later joined to bring their idea to life.

A placeholder name became a permanent one. Was it a bucket-and-playing-cards game? A child with a bucket and shovel? The original story never quite settles it—and that is part of its charm.
